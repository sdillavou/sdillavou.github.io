I am an experimental physicist working independently as an <a href="https://www.aria.org.uk/how-we-fund/the-creator-experience" target="_blank">ARIA R&D Creator</a>.

I build analog electronic systems with self-adjusting components. That is, `physical systems that train themselves` to perform desired tasks, rather than being adjusted by a central controller. They share this property with brains and other biological learning systems, but use only standard electronic parts. They are remarkably precise and fault-tolerant and have enormous potential as an energy-efficient machine learning hardware platform.

I used to study memory in frictional interfaces, collective effects in granular media, and machine learning as an experimental tool.
